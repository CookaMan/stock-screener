Stock Screener
Data from Yahoo Finance (unofficial; may be delayed, incomplete or wrong for individual stocks). Valuation ratios and margins are trailing twelve months. P/FCF uses trailing free cash flow and is N/A when it is negative. LT debt/equity uses the latest quarterly balance sheet and is N/A when shareholder equity is negative. EPS growth 3Y is annualised over the four fiscal years Yahoo publishes; a 5-year figure is not available from this source. Analyst rating runs from 1 (strong buy) to 5 (sell).
